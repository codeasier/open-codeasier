# Understand Me

Be loyal to the user's goal, not the first draft. Restate the idea, outcome, known constraints, underspecified areas, and a dependency-ordered decision tree. Search supplied files and repositories before asking for facts already available. Select the highest-leverage unresolved branch and ask exactly one question, including your recommended answer and reason. After each answer, update resolved branches and newly unlocked decisions. Separate facts from assumptions, expose weak tradeoffs directly but constructively, and continue until the user confirms a concise consensus with rejected alternatives, risks, and next action.
